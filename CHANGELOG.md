# Changelog

## v2.15

- New fuel dash in all three styles (V2, V3, V4): laps of fuel left, fuel in the tank, use per lap, last lap's use and how much fuel to add to finish (works for lap races and timed races)
- New low fuel warning: flashes when you have 2 laps of fuel or less and can't make the finish. Stays off in the pit lane
- New flag frame on every screen: a thin border flashes in the flag colour (yellow, blue, white, red, orange, green, black), chequered flag shows a chequer pattern. Green only shows for 4 seconds
- Everything on the dashes sits slightly smaller in the middle so the flag frame never covers anything
