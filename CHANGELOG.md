# Changelog

## v2.17

- New Lap History screen: your last 10 laps as bars vs your session best (purple best, green close, yellow slower, INV invalid, PIT out / in laps), plus consistency, average and best lap
- Inputs, Tires and Brakes and Lap History now come in V2 and V3 too, not just V4
- New All Screens dash for V2, V3 and V4: every screen of that style in one dash, cycle through them with a button (SimHub Controls and events > Dash Studio > Next screen / Previous screen)
- New idle screen in every dash: your name big, time and date, and a last session card (track, car, sim, best lap, laps, consistency). Set your name in idle_settings.js

## v2.16

- New Tires and Brakes screen (V4): inner / middle / outer tire temps as colour bars (blue cold, teal optimal, yellow warm, red hot), pressure, wear and brake temp on all four tires
- Brake bias, compound and front / rear brake temps in the middle
- Flag frame like the rest of the pack
- iRacing only updates tire temps when you pit, the screen shows a note

## v2.15

- New fuel dash in all three styles (V2, V3, V4): laps of fuel left, fuel in the tank, use per lap, last lap's use and how much fuel to add to finish (works for lap races and timed races)
- New low fuel warning: flashes when you have 2 laps of fuel or less and can't make the finish. Stays off in the pit lane
- New flag frame on every screen: a thin border flashes in the flag colour (yellow, blue, white, red, orange, green, black), chequered flag shows a chequer pattern. Green only shows for 4 seconds
- Everything on the dashes sits slightly smaller in the middle so the flag frame never covers anything
